# Changelog

## v1.0.3 (2026-11-19)

Refined design: buttons respond as you press them, larger history and Original controls, and item details shown on the preview card.

## v1.0.2 (2026-11-18)

You can now edit text in the preview. Edits are copied to the clipboard as you type and case buttons apply to the edited text.

## v1.0.1 (2026-11-16)

The menu bar icon shows a dot when an update is available, and the settings menu shows the current version.

## v1.0.0 (2026-11-16)

First release: case conversion, clipboard OCR, history, drag-and-drop and paste, and automatic updates.
